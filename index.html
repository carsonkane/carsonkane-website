Tax-Deductible Professional Training in Germany

Did you know language training is tax-advantaged in Germany? Under § 3 Nr. 19 EStG (Steuerfreie Weiterbildung), employer-funded professional language upskilling is 100% tax-free for German startups and companies, with zero payroll tax overhead. For freelancers and self-employed professionals, training fees are fully deductible as business expenses (Betriebsausgaben under § 4 Abs. 4 EStG or Werbungskosten under § 9 EStG). Invoices are issued with 0% VAT under § 19 UStG.
