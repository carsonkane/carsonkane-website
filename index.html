Tax-Deductible Professional Training in Germany

Professional language coaching is generally tax-deductible in Germany. Companies, startups, and freelancers can book training fees as ordinary business expenses (Betriebsausgaben, § 4 Abs. 4 EStG). When an employer funds job-related training for staff, the benefit is typically exempt from employee wage tax under § 3 Nr. 19 EStG. Employees paying out of pocket can usually claim job-related courses as Werbungskosten (§ 9 EStG). Invoices are issued without VAT under § 19 UStG (Kleinunternehmerregelung).
