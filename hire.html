German Tax Advantages & Invoicing Details

Language coaching is widely recognized as a legitimate professional development expense under German tax law. Both businesses and private individuals can leverage significant tax savings:

For Companies & Startups (§ 3 Nr. 19 EStG)

Under § 3 Nr. 19 EStG (Steuerfreie Weiterbildung), employer-sponsored professional language training is 100% tax-free for both the company and the employee. Upskill international team members with zero payroll tax burden.

For Freelancers & Employees (§ 4 Abs. 4 / § 9 EStG)

Freelancers can deduct language lessons as operational business expenses (Betriebsausgaben under § 4 Abs. 4 EStG). Employed professionals can claim fees as professional training deductions (Werbungskosten under § 9 EStG) on their annual tax return.

Invoicing Notice: Invoices are legally compliant and issued under § 19 UStG (German Small Business Regulation / Kleinunternehmerregelung) with 0% VAT.
